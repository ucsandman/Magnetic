# Magnetic — PRODUCT.md

## Register

product — the design serves the tool. Dense, calm, professional desktop app UI.

## What it is

A Windows desktop video editor (Electron + React) modeled on Final Cut Pro:
magnetic timeline, source/sequence viewer, filmstrip browser, inspector,
edit-by-transcript, silence removal, captions, smart-render export. Built for
long-form creator workflows (multi-hour OBS recordings cut into YouTube VODs
with music beds).

## Users

A single power user (the owner) editing their own recordings, keyboard-first
(JKL, blade, trim), often hours-long sessions in a dark room. Future users:
creators familiar with FCP/Premiere conventions.

## Brand personality

"Finished Apple pro app": restrained, precise, quiet confidence. The UI
disappears behind the footage. Every pixel aligned; nothing decorative.
Direct lineage: Final Cut Pro's dark chrome, SF-style small dense type,
Apple dark-mode system blue as the single accent.

## Anti-references

- Consumer-creator flashiness (CapCut gradients, badges, emoji)
- Web-app looseness: oversized paddings, rounded-everything, card grids
- Engagement-y empty states with illustrations

## Strategic design principles

1. Footage is the hero — chrome stays near-black, low contrast until focused.
2. Density over whitespace: this is a pro tool; 11–13px type is intentional.
3. One accent color (system blue) means selection/active. Nothing else is blue.
4. Every interactive control has hover, active, focus-visible, and disabled
   states that read at a glance in the dark theme.
5. Keyboard-first: visible focus, shortcut hints where FCP shows them.
